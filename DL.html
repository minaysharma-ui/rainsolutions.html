No drips. No wet belongings. Zero leaks onto your backpack. Featuring an innovative inner sponge core drainage system.

Engineered to Protect Your Belongings

🔄 Inside-Out Mechanics

Closes inversely to trap residual rainwater securely inside the canopy rather than letting it pool and slide off onto you.

🧽 Sponge Core Absorption

High-density, replaceable composite sponges integrated directly within the handle track actively soak up water runoff.

🎒 Backpack Safety Zone

Specially engineered top cap guarantees a 100% leak-proof peak, completely preventing drops from dripping down onto your gear.

The Drainage System Process

1

Inverted Canopy Collection

When the rain starts, the uniquely angled canopy channels incoming water inwards toward the structural support core rather than shedding it outward.

2

Internal Pipe Drainage

Water safely bypasses the exterior surface and moves smoothly down the inside of the hollowed center pipe line.

3

Sponge Filtration & Capture

Specially placed internal sponges comfortably house the gathered liquid, locking it away until you are ready to squeeze it clean.
